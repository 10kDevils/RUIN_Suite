## RUIN Companion App 0.8.4

**Core 0.8.4**
- **A larger RUIN window**: it opens at what couch mode used to give, and couch mode now goes larger still, never bigger than your screen.
- **Tooltips read as even blocks**, with no single word left alone on the last line.

**Codex 0.8.2**
- **Best in Slot tab** (new in 0.8.1): your character with the best piece for every slot at your level, now including **weapons and armour from quests you can still do**, as well as dungeon loot.
- **Every boss's loot in WoW Forever's new dungeons, with item IDs**: Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site, City of Dalaran, Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace, plus trash gear and boss tactics.
- **Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit** are in the Journal ahead of their December 9 opening.
- **New ranks onto your bars**: after a class trainer, one click puts the ranks you learned on your action bars.

**Pathfinder 0.8.4**
- **Quest data from November 19**: 749 new and 535 updated WoW Forever quests, with newer levels and quest givers.
- **Dungeon routes for Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace**, picking every quest up before you travel and the ones inside last.
- **Leaner routes**: no duplicate, endgame, profession or Craftsman's Writ quests in zone routes, each route within about six levels, and tidier hand-written routes.
- **A shopping list on your route**: items a quest needs are bought before you leave the city that sells them.
- **Minimap addon buttons can sit on the square frame**, notifications stay 15 seconds by default (25 for Longer), and starting a route picks it in the Guide tab.

**Exchange 0.8.4**
- **Price per Point** is the cost of one craft divided by your chance of a point (with Working Overtime), with reagents at market prices; hover it to see how it's worked out.
- **The AH price follows the auction house**, and Craft counts how many a recipe really makes.

**Finder 0.8.2**
- **Lock tag locks the tag** again on the Where page.

**Muster 0.8.1**
- **Bounties**: anyone in the org can post one, and a bounty target wears a crimson skull seal on their nameplate and portrait.
