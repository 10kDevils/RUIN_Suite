## RUIN Companion App 0.8.9

**Core 0.8.9**
- **Promote, Demote and Remove work again**, through the game's own commands; Remove takes two clicks.
- **Searches say how many they show** in Roster and People.
- **Map marks keep one size on OpenMap's map**, shadow included, and stay on their zones.
- **Clear map marks**: right-click a quest or waypoint mark, or type `/ruin clearmap`.
- **New data keys for leavers far less often** (at most every six hours).

**Pathfinder 0.8.9**
- **Routes keep to their levels**: quests more than 3 levels above a route are left out, and endgame set quests no longer show in zone routes.
- **The minimap N keeps working in dungeons.**
- **Quest marks clear themselves** once you take or hand in the quest.
- **Waypoints**: the distance sits under the card, and the arrows follow your style's accent.

**Codex 0.8.9**
- **Preview your best gear at a higher level** with a slider on the Best in Slot tab.
- **A gold check for gear that's still best at level 60.**
- **Best in Slot no longer stutters the game.**
- **Quest rewards checked against ForeverDB's complete lists.**

**Exchange 0.8.9**
- **The auction house is scanned the whole time it's open**, crafting reagents first, then gear you can use, then everything else, and every price is shared with the org. The Scan & Share button is gone.
- **Tidier tooltip lines.**

**Finder 0.8.9, Muster 0.8.9**: version matched to the suite; no changes.
