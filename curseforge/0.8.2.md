## RUIN Companion App 0.8.2

**Core 0.8.2**
- **A native Blizzard window, now the default.** The RUIN window is built from the game's own parts: the portrait window with the open module's badge and name, large module buttons across the top in the style of the Looking for Group window, the game's buttons, check boxes, input boxes, scroll bars and tabs, and its dropdown menus. Only RUIN's icons keep each module's color. Settings > Options > Window style switches back to RUIN's navy look.
- **Fewer frame stutters**: long encrypted messages are sealed one at a time as they go out, and the guild roster is built and read far less often.
- **/ruin perf** lists the addons with the longest frames since login.

**Pathfinder 0.8.2**
- **Fewer frame stutters**: quest reminders read your quest log once and rebuild the zone's list far less often.

**Exchange 0.8.1**
- **Soulbound items are never sent to the auction house.** The tooltip and the bag sweep weigh only disenchanting, crafting and the vendor for them, and recommend whichever is worth most.

**Finder 0.8.1**
- In the Blizzard window style, Muster events on the board are labeled in the game's gold.
