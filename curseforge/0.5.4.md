## RUIN Companion App 0.5.4

**Fixes**
- Far fewer "You're doing that too fast" errors: item lookups are queued, RUIN's messages leave room for other addons, and RUIN backs off when the server complains. `/ruin toofast` shows what caused the last ten.
- Items no longer stick at "Loading item..." on WoW Forever's client.
- The party dungeon strip hides inside dungeons and raids.
- Officers who just joined get the officer key without waiting.

**Exchange**
- Reads WoW Forever's auction house, with an automatic full scan when you open it (`/ex scan`, `/ex autoscan`).
- Price per Point beside every recipe in the profession window, using the skill-up chance for orange, yellow and green. Unlearned recipes are priced at the trainer.
- Recipe item tooltips show Price per Craft and Price per Point.
- Vendor prices, used only when cheaper than the auction house and always marked as vendor.

**Pathfinder**
- A quest route for every zone (with Questie or QuestieDB), split by level.
- Routes page in Dungeons, Zones and Completed / out-leveled sections, sorted by level and foldable; checkboxes hide routes from the Guide's list.
- City of Dalaran route completed, and a new Cozy Sleeping Bag route.
- The tracker has Open and Clear buttons; `/path clear` leaves your route.

**Codex**
- Loot lists load item names a few at a time through RUIN Core.
