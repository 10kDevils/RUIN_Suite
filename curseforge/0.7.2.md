## RUIN Companion App 0.7.2

**Security (0.7.2)**
- **The org key is signed by the admin.** Only the admin's account can change it; officers pass keys on but can't alter them (no more swapped Discord links or channels), and disbanding needs the admin's signature.
- **The admin can't be impersonated by name.** Taking a deleted or renamed admin character's name gets you nothing; the admin's client vouches for its own characters.
- **Admin recovery code** (Settings > Org > Recovery code): keeps you the org admin on a new PC or after a reinstall. Keep it private.
- **Encrypted org traffic.** Once the admin raises the org's minimum version to 0.7.2 (Settings > Org), everything on the org and officer channels and every RUIN whisper is encrypted. Only members of the org's guilds can read it, even if the key phrase leaks, and people who leave a guild are locked out.
- **Org minimum version.** It starts at 0.6.0, so nobody is locked out. RUIN below the org's minimum can't connect and is told to update through CurseForge or GitHub; anyone below the admin's version is told a newer version is out.
- **Privacy:** RUIN no longer sends anything derived from your BattleTag.

**Finder (0.7.1)**
- "Which one" is a dropdown that follows the activity: every dungeon, raid or questing zone (no capital cities), PvP (Dueling, World, Battlegrounds), Professions (Farming, Services), Social (Just Chatting, Theory Craft, RP).
- New activity **Rares** (Mobs, Items, Secrets), right below Questing.
- The Spec box starts on **Auto** (your spec from your talents, from Codex when it's installed), lists every profession for Professions and the tone (Casual, Mutual, Engaged) for Social.
- **Group size** and **Allow Queue**: a full group stops taking join requests unless it keeps a queue. Everyone asking to join shows on your listing card with Invite and Decline.
- Place the guild tag anywhere around your target frame (Finder > Where); it starts on the right side.

**Pathfinder (0.7.1)**
- **Minimap options** (Options > Minimap): round or square; the game's own look or Pathfinder's modern one (a slim ring or edge in Pathfinder's green, an N, the zone in a pill, mouse-wheel zoom).
- **Dock the tracker** to the minimap: above it grows up, below it grows down, on a side it grows away from the map.
- Mark on map in the alert window opens the world map on the quest's giver. The alert window and tracker match the new look, and the edge arrow sits right on the edge of the waypoint.
