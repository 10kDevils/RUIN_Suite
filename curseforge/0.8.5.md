## RUIN Companion App 0.8.5

**Core 0.8.5**
- **Org chat**: `/org <message>` talks in a channel only your org's members can see, also in Guild > Chat > **Org chat**. It's sky blue by default; Settings > Options picks another color.
- **Org announcements**: officers and the org admin can put a message in the middle of every member's screen with the raid warning sound (`/orgwarn` or `/ow`, or **Announce** in Org chat). Settings > Options turns the screen and sound off.
- **The pop-out guild chat window resizes, locks and fades**: drag its corner to resize it, **Lock** keeps it in place, and **Opacity** fades its background while the text stays readable.
- **Map pins show on the world map with OpenMap**: Mark on Map and waypoints are drawn above OpenMap's stitched map, in the right spot.

**Pathfinder 0.8.5**
- **Quest reminders are quieter**: the reminder sound is shorter and plays at most once every 30 seconds.
