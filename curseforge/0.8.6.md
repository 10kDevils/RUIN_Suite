## RUIN Companion App 0.8.6

**Core 0.8.6**
- **Org chat stays on Org**: after `/org <message>`, your chat box keeps "Org:" for the next lines you type, like /g or /p, until you switch with another chat command.
- **Org chat shows full names**, as guild chat does.
- **Org chat is the same blue as the [RUIN] tag** by default; a color you picked in Settings stays.
