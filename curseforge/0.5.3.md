## RUIN Companion App 0.5.3

**Fixes**
- No more Disable / Ignore popup for RUIN Core on every reload in WoW Forever.
- Fixed "You're doing that too fast" when hovering players.

**Codex**
- The Dungeon Journal is the first tab and covers every classic dungeon and raid, including The Stockade.
- Full loot list for each dungeon, read live from QuestieDB or Questie when installed.
- Each dungeon lists its quests for your race and class, with a Quest route button that opens the route in Pathfinder.
- Boss kill tracker and a General section for the greens and blues that drop from trash.
- Click an item for its full tooltip. Sections fold, and quests start folded.
- Two-handers are compared against main hand plus off hand. With Ruinous Forever Planner loaded, the planner handles gear comparison.

**Pathfinder**
- Dungeon quest routes, including the quests that unlock them.
- Routes updated from the WoW Forever launch data.

**Exchange**
- Crafters shows one row per recipe, led by the online member with the lowest skill, plus an Offline crafters switch.

**Finder and Muster**
- Click a census mark on a continent map to open that zone. The zone's own map shows it in the bottom-right corner.
- Muster's event, type and size pickers open as lists.

**Everywhere**
- Gold accents on buttons, toggles, tabs and headers. Buttons that open another addon use that addon's color.
- Every dropdown-style button opens a real list.
- Smaller party dungeon strip with a Run bar under each dungeon worth running, and floating windows move as soon as you press.
