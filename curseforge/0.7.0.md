## RUIN Companion App 0.7.0

**A new look for the whole suite**
- Flat tabs with RUIN's own icons (a magnifying glass for Finder, a rally flag for Muster, a compass for Pathfinder, an open book for Codex, scales for Exchange, a gear for Settings).
- Every page header carries a drawing of what the page is about, washed in its color.
- Buttons take the page's color when you use them, check boxes show a real check mark, scroll bars are slim and menus are cleaner.

**Pathfinder**
- **Quest data built in:** every WoW Forever quest with its givers, targets and hand-ins ships inside Pathfinder, so routes, waypoints and tooltips work with nothing else installed. If you still have a `RUIN_QuestData` folder, you can delete it.
- **Quests in your log:** the Guide's first route plans the best order to finish every quest you have, from where you stand.
- **A route planner that walks less:** givers picked up together, hand-ins gathered into one visit, and quests that share targets done in one step.
- **Pick-up reminders** when you walk past your route's next quest giver, or into a quest's targets without the quest, in a small alert window with Mark on map.
- **The atlas** learns where RUIN members really pick up and hand in quests on WoW Forever.
- **The Waypoint Editor** holds everything about the waypoint: what it shows, its position, when it hides, and how close to each screen edge it moves to the edge (10% on every side to start). Its card shows at the screen edge too.
- Tabs are now Quests, Guide, Routes, Record, Options; In-Game Navigation switches the game's navigation off as well as on; the original key bindings are back.

**Codex**
- Auto spec reads your talent points, so the upgrade line weighs the spec you actually play.
- A one-hander is compared with your best off hand against a wielded two-hander.
- Spell math (mana per point, DPS or HPS) shows on macros too.

**Exchange**
- Keeps the lowest buyout, reading every listing.
- The tooltip's advice is colored: Sell to Vendor green, Sell on AH gold, Craft or Disenchant red.
- The Working Overtime Legacy talent counts in skill-up costs (Leveling page).

**Core**
- The org admin can hand the admin to another player; anyone can leave the org and the admin can disband it.
- What RUIN shares about you: switch off each thing RUIN shares.
- A safer officer channel, with a lock-out for demoted officers; shared prices, quest places, drop counts and crafts are checked before they count.
- Community links are read however you write them and reach every member online at once.
