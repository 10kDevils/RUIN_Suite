## RUIN Companion App 0.7.3

**Core 0.7.3**
- **New-version prompts.** When RUIN hears that a newer version is out, it tells you in chat and, once per version, in a window: update through CurseForge or GitHub.
- **Lock-out notices come from RUIN.** A RUIN below the org's minimum version is told by RUIN itself and shown the update window; nobody's character whispers anyone.

**Pathfinder 0.7.5**
- **Fixed: the round minimap went see-through** with the minimap options on. Pathfinder leaves the game's round map alone and ships its own round mask for switching back from square.
- **Fixed: the square minimap stayed round** with the game's round border. The square shape now sticks, and the round border stays hidden.
- **Fixed: Pathfinder's square style had a dark layer over the map.** Its shadow now sits outside the edge.
- `/path minimap debug` lists what's drawn around the minimap.
