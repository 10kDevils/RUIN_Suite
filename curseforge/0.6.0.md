## RUIN Companion App 0.6.0

**Pathfinder**
- A RUIN waypoint pin stands over wherever you're headed. It shows the quest or step, its objectives, the distance and the walking time, waits at the screen edge when the spot is off screen, and fades away inside the quest's objective area.
- The Waypoint Editor (`/path editor`) lets you choose what the pin shows and how it looks, with a live preview.
- The tracker has Route and Tracked tabs: your route, or the quest you picked in the objective tracker, with each objective on its own line. The pin follows the selected tab.
- A simpler tracker: round arrows on its sides, an x to clear, and a double-click to open Pathfinder. It grows wider, then taller, so text is never cut off.
- An Options tab for the tracker, the waypoint and the pin, plus new key bindings.
- Pathfinder never changes the game's tracking in combat, and it respects quests you untrack.

**Exchange**
- Every item tooltip ends with a boxed RUIN: Exchange section: the AH price for the stack, a cheaper merchant (named by kind, e.g. "Trade Goods NPC"), the 7-day average, and what to do with the item.
- It recommends one of four things:
  - **Sell on AH**, after the 1 silver deposit and the 5% cut
  - **Sell to Vendor**
  - **Disenchant**, when the mats are worth more than selling
  - **Craft**, when one of your recipes turns a reagent into something that sells for more
- A full AH scan records the prices of the items in your bags first.

**Codex**
- Spell tooltips show the mana per expected point of damage or healing, and the expected DPS or HPS. Crits are counted, including on damage and healing over time.

**Core**
- Money is shown with the game's gold, silver and copper coin icons.
- Map and minimap pins are round, on a soft shadow.
