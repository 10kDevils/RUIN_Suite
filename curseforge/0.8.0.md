## RUIN Companion App 0.8.0

**Pathfinder: your game, your look**
- **A Customization tab** (the Options tab, renamed, now scrolling). Game Frames comes first, then Tracker, Waypoint and Controls.
- **Eight looks** for the minimap, the objective tracker, the world map and quest log, the tracker and the waypoint:
  - Blizzard (the game's own)
  - RUIN (the app's navy and gold)
  - Ink (gold on near-black)
  - Pathfinder, Finder, Muster, Codex and Exchange (each module's color)
- **Background opacity** for each frame. All styles sets every frame at once, with one opacity for all.
- **A modern minimap**: a flat frame around the ring holds the tracking button, zone and coordinates, calendar, day/night icon, mail and clock in its corners. It has a clear N that turns with a rotating map, and it fills its Edit Mode box.
- **A restyled objective tracker and quest log**: flat quest markers and icons, quest titles that stand out from objectives, and zone headers that stand out in every look.
- **The waypoint**:
  - Travel time follows your speed: on foot, mounted, swimming, flying or with a speed boost, by road on the ground and along the flight path in the air.
  - It rises toward the horizon over faraway spots.
  - It has a Blizzard style in WoW Forever's bronze.
- **Mark on Map and View Location** on the Quests page. View Location tracks the quest and opens your map on it.

**Codex**
- **Scarlet Monastery is four dungeons**: Graveyard, Library, Armory and Cathedral, each with its own levels, bosses and loot.
- **Bosses stand apart**, each in its own band with its drop count and kills. Rare bosses are tagged.
- **A loot filter**: an armor or weapon type, one-hand, two-hand or ranged, cloaks, jewelry, what your class can use, or upgrades for you.
- **Green arrows on upgrades** beside item names, from Ruinous Forever Planner when it's installed, otherwise from Codex's stat weights. Only gear your class can use gets one.
- **Dungeons sort by level** (or A-Z). Raids are grouped by 10, 20 and 40 players.
- **Stat weights rechecked for every spec**:
  - Weapon damage counts at its real worth.
  - Casters value Intellect, Spirit and mana per 5 more while leveling.
  - The enchant on what you wear counts in a comparison.

**Finder**
- **Link in chat**: put a link to your listing in chat. Anyone with Finder can click it to open the board on your listing. A link only appears for the player's own listing.

**Muster**
- **Events go on the Guild Calendar too**, when your guild rank may add guild events. A box on the New event form turns this off.
- **Slayers works on WoW Forever**: honorable kills are counted now.
- **Wanted is the first tab.**

**Exchange**
- **The tooltip's AH price is the low end** of the last week's prices, leaving out mispriced listings. A dearer listing on the auction house now is shown as *now*.
- Price history for items you stopped seeing is cleared at login.

**Core**
- **A Blizzard window style** for the whole RUIN window (Settings > Options > Window style). Each module keeps its color for accents.
- **Compact tooltips** that open beside what you point at, everywhere in the suite.
- **Drop-down lists** can open past the window's edge.
- **Options comes first** in Settings.
- **/ruin errors** lists RUIN's own recent Lua errors.

**Lighter and steadier**
- No more Lua errors in combat from values the game hides.
- Much less work every frame and every second: the waypoint, the minimap, the objective tracker and the message queue.
