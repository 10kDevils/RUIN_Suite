## RUIN Companion App 0.9.0

**Core 0.9.0**
- **Settings > Customization.** The looks for the minimap, objective tracker, world map and quest log moved here from Pathfinder (so they work without it), with Pathfinder's Tracker and Waypoint sections when it's installed.
- **Setup guide for guild leaders** starting an org: create it, add guilds, send keys, bring members in.
- **No more "No player named ... is currently playing" spam** when keys change; key offers you send always go out.
- **Lighter while RUIN talks to the org**, and distances read "here" when you're there.

**Pathfinder 0.9.0**
- **New Alliance 1-60 levelling routes** planned for the most experience an hour, each ending with the next one to three routes. Starting areas stay in your race's zone until its quests run out.
- **Suggested for you**, route names that start with their levels, lists sorted by level.
- **Session time, class quests, a Group button and Follow my party leader.**
- **Quests from dropped items** mark where the item drops.

**Finder 0.9.0**
- **Join replaces Layers**: ask a member in your zone to invite you to reach their layer, or ask anyone here.

**Muster 0.9.0**
- **Attendance** per player, **reminders** before events you signed up for, and the rally point always shows on event rows.

**Codex 0.9.0**
- **The best quest reward for you** is marked when a quest offers a choice, and your buyable Best in Slot pieces feed Exchange's Watch list.

**Exchange 0.9.0**
- **Watch list** for items you want to buy, and **Waylaid Crates** list each bundle with its auction house price.
