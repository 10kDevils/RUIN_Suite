What it does

Every member's market knowledge, pooled

Shared prices

Browse the auction house as usual. When you close it, the prices you saw go to all of RUIN, so everyone's tooltips stay fresh without anyone scanning.

Crafter directory

Opening a profession window shares your recipes. Find: Crafters lists them by profession: each item in its quality color, the RUIN member who can craft it, and its quality. Whisper them in one click.

Sell or vendor

Tooltips say when an item sells for clearly more on the auction house than the vendor pays, and when it isn't worth the trip.

In the RUIN window

Who can make it?

One search box for crafters and prices. Type part of a recipe or item name; results update as you type.

What it shares with RUIN

Prices and recipes

The lowest unit price you saw for each item while browsing, sent in batches when you close the auction house, and your recipe list each time you open a profession. Your gold and your bags stay private.

New in 0.3.6

  • At login Exchange shares the recipes it already knows and asks for any profession it hasn't read yet: one click opens, reads and closes it
  • Enchanting is read too, on clients where it has its own window

New in 0.3.5

  • Crafters listed by profession, then recipe, the member who crafts it, and the item's quality, with a profession filter
  • Items show their real names everywhere, never "item #1234"

New in 0.3.4

  • Search reads Find: Crafters or Find: Prices, and every box shows what to type in it, in Exchange's gold

New in 0.3.3

  • Its tab sits in the RUIN window in the order Guild, Finder, Muster, Pathfinder, Codex, Exchange, Settings

New in 0.3.1

  • The RUIN Gaming Companion App look: a navy window, the gold RUIN mark and tabs across the top
  • Loads on Retail as well as WoW Forever, with settings kept per game

New in 0.3.0

  • Price history with a 7-day trend on tooltips
  • The cheapest way to level your profession, priced with live RUIN data
  • A craft request board that alerts crafters who know the recipe
  • A bag sweep that labels every item keep, sell or vendor

Prices come only from what members browse. There is no full-scan button.
