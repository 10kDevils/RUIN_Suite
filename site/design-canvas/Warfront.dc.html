How it plays

Name it, take it, hold the line

Warfront gives RUIN's world PvP a purpose: places that matter tonight, chosen by your officers, with the whole org pointed at them.

1

Officers mark the ground

An officer stands on a crossroads, a flight master or a rival's farming spot and presses Mark objective here.

2

The org is alerted

Every RUIN member gets an alert, and the HUD's arrow points to the nearest objective, turning as you turn, with the distance beside it.

3

Stand together to capture

As you close in, a radar shows the circle around you. Enough RUIN members inside it for a full minute takes it, and the guild with the most fighters present gets the credit.

4

Call to Arms

Jumped while questing? One press tells every RUIN member where you are, and their HUD points to you for two minutes.

Capture rules

A capture takes a team

2+ membersNeeded inside the circle at once, so one player can't fake a capture. Officers can raise the number.
60 secondsHeld continuously. Step out and the timer resets.
Most presentThe guild with the most members in the circle is credited with the capture.
2 hoursObjectives expire on their own, so the map stays about tonight.

Where it lives

Built for the fight, not the menu

On screen
Warfront HUD

An arrow to the nearest objective or call for help, a radar near the circle, capture progress, and a Help! button.

Key binding
Call to Arms

Bind it to a Steam Deck back button. No menu between you and help.

Slash commands
/wf mark <name> · /wf help

Officers mark from chat; anyone can call for help.

RUIN window
Warfront page

Every objective with status, distance and who set it, under a war-red header of crossed swords. Officers can remove them; anyone can switch on practice mode.

What it shares with RUIN

Position only when it counts

Warfront only sends your position when you are standing in an objective's circle, press Call to Arms, or claim a bounty kill. Officers who log in later receive the current objectives from officers already online.

New in 0.3.0

  • Objective and Call to Arms pins on the world map and minimap
  • Bounties on rival guilds or players, confirmed by a second RUIN witness
  • A weekly war record and leaderboard across RUIN's guilds
  • Contested objectives when Intel reports enemies nearby

To try it alone, tick Practice mode on the Warfront page or type /wf practice: mark objectives, capture them and post bounties by yourself. Practice items never leave your screen.
