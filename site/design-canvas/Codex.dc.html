What it does

Three answers before every run

The journal

Every dungeon's level range, where it is, and each boss with a one-line tip on what to watch for. Inside a dungeon, Codex opens on that dungeon.

Learned drops

When anyone in RUIN loots an uncommon or better item in a dungeon, it's added to that dungeon's list for everyone. The org builds the loot table by playing.

The upgrade line

Hover any gear and Codex compares it with what you're wearing and names the slot: "+18% upgrade over your chest", or "-8% vs your ring 2". For rings and trinkets it compares against the weaker of your two.

What it shares with RUIN

Your loot, never twice

Only items you loot yourself inside a dungeon, with the dungeon's name. Because each member reports only their own loot, a group of five doesn't count one drop five times.
