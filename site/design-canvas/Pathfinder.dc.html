How it plays

Accept, do, hand in. It keeps up.

Pick a route and press Start. Steps that name a quest tick off when you accept it, finish it and turn it in. The arrows move by hand for anything else.

New in 0.3.13 · Questie and shared quests

Your quest log, with RUIN beside you

With Questie installed, Pathfinder reads its quest database while you play: where each quest starts and ends, where its targets are, its level and objectives. The new Quests page lists your log and, for every quest, who in RUIN has it too.

Every step on the map

Steps without a place get one from Questie: the quest giver, the hand-in, or the nearest spot of the targets. Hover a step for Questie's details.

Skip what you've done

Steps for quests you've already finished, or already have, tick themselves off, so a route picks up wherever you are.

Who has it

"Westfall Stew: Cara and Eve have it, Eve is ready to hand in." Show it on the map, or invite them with one button. Steps others are on say "2 in RUIN".

Draft a route

One button makes a route of the quests Questie lists for your zone and level, ready to start or polish with the recorder.

The built-in Alliance routes were checked against the WoW Forever quest database: every quest step carries its quest ID, wrong givers and broken chains were fixed (Elwynn, Westfall, Dun Morogh, Duskwood, Redridge on Forever's redrawn map), and missing quests were added. Nothing of Questie's is copied into RUIN.

What it shares with RUIN

Your place on the route

The route you chose and your step number, when it changes and every two minutes. The quest IDs in your log, which are ready to hand in, and which of your route's quests you've done, when they change and every five minutes (Share with RUIN turns it off).
