How it plays

One call, every guild answers

Each guild's calendar only reaches its own members. Muster posts once for all of RUIN, so a joint raid fills from every guild at the same time.

1

An officer posts

Title, time and type: Raid, Dungeon, PvP, Leveling or Social. The time is filled in with tomorrow at 20:00 to start from.

2

RUIN hears about it

Every member online gets an alert, and members who log in later receive upcoming events from whoever is online.

3

Sign up by role

Press T, H or D to join as tank, healer or DPS. X withdraws. Counts update for everyone at once.

4

Get reminded

Fifteen minutes before an event you signed up for, an alert with sound, wherever you are.

New in 0.3.3 · From the retired Warfront

Rallies and bounties

RUIN's world PvP now runs on Overlord Forever: its campaign, keeps, captures and Most Wanted. Muster adds what only RUIN does: meeting places for the org, and bounties confirmed by a witness.

1

Call a rally

Tick Rally here when you post an event, or type /muster rally where you stand. Everyone gets an alert and a pin on the world map and minimap.

2

Know when it's contested

When a sighting reports enemies within about 300 yards, the rally is marked CONTESTED for ten minutes and the org is told.

3

Post a bounty

Officers put a price on a rival guild or player. It reaches every RUIN member and lasts a week.

4

Claim with a witness

Kill one, target the body and claim, by button, /muster claim or a key binding. Another RUIN member nearby confirms it.

In the RUIN window

The week ahead at a glance

Officers see the post form at the top; everyone sees upcoming events with role counts and their own sign-up.

New in 0.3.4 · From the retired Intel

Sightings and timers

Muster already reacts to what happens in the world, so RUIN Intel folded into it. See something, press one button, and every RUIN member gets the alert.

Report what you see

Enemy group, under attack, world boss, rare, buff drop or other, with your location. Type /muster spot, or /intel, or bind a key for enemy groups and for under attack.

Alerts and pins

Everyone gets an alert and a pin on the world map and minimap that fades over 30 minutes. The Sightings page keeps the feed.

Find group

Boss, rare and enemy sightings have a Find group button that lists you in RUIN Finder for that activity. That is the one part of Intel that belongs to Finder.

World boss and buff timers

The Timers page: press Now when a boss dies or a buff drops, or announce a drop in a few minutes. Shared with the org and re-sent to anyone who logs in.
