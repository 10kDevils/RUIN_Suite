How it plays

From one pair of eyes to the whole org

On a PvP ruleset, what you know first decides the fight. Intel turns every RUIN member into a scout without anyone typing coordinates into chat.

1

See it

An enemy raid crossing the road, a world boss up, a rare, a buff about to drop.

2

Press one button

Pick what you saw and add a few words if you want. Your exact location is filled in for you.

3

Everyone knows

Every RUIN member gets an alert, with sound for enemies, bosses and buff drops.

4

It stays on the board

Reports stay in the Intel feed for 30 minutes, so latecomers see what's been happening.

Five kinds of report

Color-coded, so you can react without reading

Enemy groupHorde on the move. Alert with sound.
World bossUp and spotted. Alert with sound.
RareA rare spawn worth the trip. Quiet alert.
Buff dropA world buff about to go up. Alert with sound.
OtherAnything else, in your own words. Quiet alert.

Each kind of alert can be switched off on the Intel page. Reports still land in the feed.

What it shares with RUIN

Only what you report

Each report you make, with its kind, your note and your location at that moment. Nothing is sent unless you press a button, and nothing is kept between sessions.
