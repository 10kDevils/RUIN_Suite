How it plays

From "anyone?" to a full group

No trade-chat spam and no hoping the right people are online in your own guild. Finder pools every RUIN guild into one board.

1

Flag yourself

Tick I'm looking, pick your roles, choose an activity and say where: Dungeon, Raid, PvP, Questing, Professions or Social.

2

Show up everywhere

Your listing appears on the board of every RUIN member online, with your class, level, spec and note.

3

Group up

Whisper or invite anyone on the board in one press. Filter to the activity you care about.

4

Get nudged

While you're looking, an alert tells you the moment someone else starts looking for the same thing.

In the RUIN window

Your status on top, the org below

Finder adds one page to the RUIN window. Your own listing sits at the top of the board, marked You, so you see exactly what the org sees, and a status line says when it is live.

Where it lives

Finder page

Your status and the org-wide board, under a rose header of pinned notes.

Slash command
/finder on dungeon

Start or stop looking without opening anything. /finder off to stop.

Key bindings
Open Finder · Toggle looking

Map them to Steam Deck buttons for couch play.

On screen
Alerts

A short alert at the top of the screen when someone matches your activity.

What it shares with RUIN

A small beacon, nothing more

While you're looking, Finder sends a short beacon on the org channel when you change something and every two minutes. Listings disappear five minutes after the last beacon, so the board only shows people who are actually online and looking.

New in 0.3.0

  • Automatic matching: players and groups that fit each other both get an alert, and matches sort to the top

  • Level-range filters, and Muster events with open slots listed on the board
