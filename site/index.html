Why Core exists

Your guild chat stops at your guild. RUIN doesn't.

RUIN is several Alliance guilds fighting under one name. The game only knows about each guild on its own, so Core supplies the part the game is missing: one organization that every guild belongs to.

Guilds are islands

Guild chat, officer chat and the roster all end at the edge of your guild. Members of the other RUIN guilds are strangers to the game, even when you raid with them every week.

Forever has no realms

WoW Forever puts everyone on one huge ruleset with layering. There is no small realm where everyone knows RUIN, so the org has to hold itself together.

Every addon needs the same plumbing

Finder, Warfront, Codex and the rest all need to reach every RUIN member. Core builds that connection once, so each module can stay small and optional.

Setup

Raise the banner in three steps

Done once for the whole org. Members don't type anything.

1

The org admin creates the key

RUIN's main guild master opens /ruin, presses Create org key, then Add guild for each RUIN guild with its officer rank.

2

Send it to each guild leader

Target the other guild's leader and press Send key to target. They see an Accept prompt. No copying and pasting.

3

Members get it automatically

Inside each guild, officers share the key with members as they log in. Officers also receive the officer channel.

Commands

Everything starts with /ruin

CommandWhat it does
/ruinOpen or close the RUIN window. Also on a key binding and the addon compartment.
/ruin statusConnection status: org, channels, bridge, members online.
/ruin versionsWhich RUIN versions are running across the org.
/ruin key send <name>Offer the org key to another guild's leader.
/ruin key rotateOrg admin: new channel passwords for everyone, to lock out anyone who left.
/ruin bridge on | offAllow or stop relaying for guildmates without the addon.
/ruin couchCouch mode: the window at 115% for Steam Deck and TV play.
